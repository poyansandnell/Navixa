## Description (English)
SINK SHIPS. CLIMB THE RANKS. BECOME ADMIRAL.

Navixa is the classic naval battle game — rebuilt as a modern online experience. Place your fleet, outsmart your opponent, and sink ship after ship in tactical duels against players worldwide.

YOUR PACE, YOUR RULES
• Blitz — intense real-time battles when you have five minutes to spare
• Daily matches — up to 24 hours per move, play several matches at once and get notified when it's your turn

CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
• Create a private match and share a code or link — via SMS, Messenger, anything
• Sync contacts (optional, privacy-safe) to find friends who already play
• See when your friends are online

CLIMB THE RANKS
• Ranked matches with a real rating system — every win counts
• Leaderboards: global, friends, and nearby
• Weekly tournaments with brackets and prizes

FAIR PLAY
• The server judges every move — no cheating, no shortcuts
• Report and block disruptive players

COLLECT & PROGRESS
• Daily quests and rewards
• Customize your fleet in the shop
• Full match history with move-by-move replay

Ready to set sail? Your fleet awaits, captain.
